Off-thread prepare, immutable prepared coefficients, no runtime allocation/deletion or locks in request/tick; single audio-thread owner. Cannot reprepare concurrently with audio. Input rate supplied by caller; no resampling or WAV loading. Wet-only, zero fixed buffering (deliberate IR onset remains).
