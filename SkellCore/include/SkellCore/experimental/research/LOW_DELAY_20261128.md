## Low-latency policy for consumer adoption
Recording: target no intentional lookahead/fixed buffering for ordinary EQ, dynamics, modulation and direct/causal coloration, with actual group-delay and driver/host latency measured separately. Use the new IIR as opt-in after listening and per-consumer headroom checks, not a transparent replacement. Pitch correction and spectral algorithms have analysis-window costs; do not claim zero latency where impossible. Convolution retains deliberate wet delay/IR tail, not added engine buffering when tested. Protection: 0-sample existing sample-peak limiter or optional 16-sample finite-kernel guard when explicitly requested. Mixing/mastering: retain existing high-quality FIR modes, report and align their exact integer delay. Do not dynamically change a plugin's reported latency during playback without host-supported transition tests; no hidden extra latency, no changes to approved defaults.
