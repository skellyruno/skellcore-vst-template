## Reusable engines and provenance

- modulation/StatefulStereoMod.h is an exact upstream blob (4c4a0699e350f87c03aafb27289c01a6df7bb684), retaining namespace skell::research; adapted include-only test. Single audio-thread owner; eight controls smoothly slewed with persistent stereo history and bounded signed nonlinear feedback. 128 KiB stereo ring, zero added algorithmic buffering. Not the independent Chorus fixture's float32 clock. Upstream stress peak 1.9168 and one native 192k/64 callback miss; feedback aliasing, headroom, sample-accurate host automation bridge and structural switching remain unverified.
- dynamics/DetectorCascade.h is an original stateful adaptation of StereoDetectorProbe.cpp (770b78313f32e19bf9cbf206b32ebb14f579f834) and CascadeIdentification.cpp (be1bf6d8782d5985c0c661aaf76f5f62ffe75393). One/two feedforward amplitude-detector stages, per-stage link in [0,1], explicit slope and effective attack/release seconds, cached poles, per-channel memory, no added buffering. No vendor control calibration or general topology claim. Prepare-time configuration only; prepare/reset clears memory. Input finite guard and [-4,4] bound; tiny detector states flush. Not an automatic replacement for OriginalAdaptiveCompressor or existing compressors.
- spectral/CausalHalfbandColor.h adapts HalfbandColor.cpp (4677278be8bee486bcd6c5bf565a55b37973d732) into persistent stereo fixed storage; coefficient arrays are owned, not external pointers. CausalHalfbandCoefficients.h captures exact original 641/65 projected symmetric remez designs from SciPy 1.17.0 with odd phase values mirrored, even zero taps except center .5. First stage pass/stop edges .49/.51 of stage Nyquist, second .3/.7, Kaiser not used in the equiripple designs. Wet-only, explicit 336-sample latency (7ms at 48k), prepare-time drive and coefficients; unprepared output silence. No dry path alignment, host latency reporting or drive automation. Prepare requires finite exact symmetry, bounded L1, but does not establish frequency response of arbitrary supplied coefficients. The original shorter candidates did not meet held-out spectral gate; retain long reference. No production adoption.
